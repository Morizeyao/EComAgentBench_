# Data Statement

## Product corpus: Amazon Reviews 2023

EComAgentBench builds its product environment from the **Amazon Reviews 2023** dataset
(McAuley Lab), hosted at
<https://huggingface.co/datasets/McAuley-Lab/Amazon-Reviews-2023>.

- We do **not** redistribute the corpus. `scripts/download_amazon_reviews.py` downloads the raw
  category files from the Hugging Face Hub, and `scripts/build_product_index.py` builds a local
  SQLite + FTS5 database at `data/products/product.db`.
- The product database used in the paper contains roughly **3.7M products** and **21M reviews**
  across **four full categories** (`All_Beauty`, `Electronics`, `Cell_Phones_and_Accessories`,
  `Office_Products`), capped at **20 reviews per product** (random seed `42`). The
  electronics-adjacent categories (electronics, phones, office) dominate the product distribution.
- Use of Amazon Reviews 2023 is subject to its original terms and is intended for **research
  purposes only**. Please cite Hou et al. (2024) when using it.

## User personas and clarification scripts

- All `user_persona` and `clarification_script` fields in the benchmark are **synthetically
  generated by an LLM** during construction. They contain **no real user data and no PII**.
- Personas are fictional and exist solely to inject hidden, tool-gated shopping constraints
  (the "distributed hidden user intent" the benchmark measures).

## Benchmark file

- `data/benchmark/benchmark.jsonl` contains the **662 validated samples**
  (`sample_status == "ok"`) used in the paper.
- Generation artifacts (`extracted_features`, `feature_partition`, `validation_*`, `judge_*`,
  etc.) are retained for transparency and reproducibility; the evaluation pipeline uses the
  `rubrics`, `info_source`, and `target_product` fields.
